# Pricing Sheet

CommerceLens pricing is usage-based, with higher credit cost for rendered pages
and catalog crawling because browser execution and page traversal are more
expensive than static extraction.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Included Usage | Best For |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Starter | $99/mo | 5,000 extraction credits, basic alerts | Small teams validating monitored competitors |
| Growth | $499/mo | 50,000 extraction credits, jobs, exports, webhooks | Operators running recurring competitor reports |
| Business | $1,500+/mo | Custom limits, higher concurrency, priority support | Pricing teams and agencies with active workflows |
| Enterprise | Custom | SLA, SSO, private deployment, custom retention | Regulated or high-volume deployments |

## Credit Weights

| Operation | Credits |
| --- | ---: |
| Static product extraction | 1 |
| Listing extraction | 2 |
| Rendered product extraction | 5 |
| Rendered listing extraction | 10 |
| Catalog crawl page | 3 |
| Monitor job target | 1-5 per run |

## Commercial Guardrails

- Use monthly domain quotas for high-risk domains.
- Price custom adapters separately when a customer requires domain-specific
  extractor work.
- Require Business or Enterprise for private deployment, custom retention, or
  guaranteed response times.

# CommerceLens Product Brief

CommerceLens monitors product, price, availability, and catalog changes across
commerce sites and turns them into structured data, alerts, and exports.

## Who It Is For

- DTC brands watching competitor price and stock movement
- Retailers monitoring pricing and catalog compliance
- Marketplaces tracking seller/catalog changes
- Ecommerce agencies producing recurring competitor reports
- Pricing teams that need alertable market signals

## Core Workflows

- Extract normalized product and listing data from commerce pages.
- Schedule monitored product groups by account and project.
- Detect price drops, price increases, availability changes, and back-in-stock
  events.
- Deliver alerts to webhooks, Slack, email, files, or API consumers.
- Review jobs, runs, extractions, usage, quotas, and failures in the customer
  portal.
- Export normalized product records for analysis or downstream systems.

## Why It Wins

CommerceLens is built around commerce objects, not raw HTML. Customers get
product names, brands, prices, currencies, availability, images, confidence,
provenance, price history, alert events, and run history from one focused API.

## Initial Offer

Hosted API plus customer portal for monitored products, alert rules, run
history, extraction records, exports, API keys, usage, and quota visibility.

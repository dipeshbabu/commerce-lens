# Community Code of Conduct

CommerceLens adopts the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as the baseline for participation in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Welcome different experience levels and
perspectives. Give actionable feedback, accept correction in good faith, and focus debate on
the work and its impact.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information, and other conduct
that would be inappropriate in a professional setting are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, reject contributions, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Actions
will be proportionate to the impact and pattern of the behavior.

Report content or accounts through GitHub's built in reporting controls when those controls
are available. To reach the repository maintainer privately, open a
[private report](https://github.com/dipeshbabu/commerce-lens/security/advisories/new), prefix
the title with `Conduct report`, and include only the information needed to investigate.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Do not retaliate against a reporter or
participant in an investigation.

This concise policy incorporates the scope and community impact principles of Contributor
Covenant 2.1. Its enforcement ladder was inspired by Mozilla's code of conduct enforcement
guidance.
